Barcode Label Generator

Product name, SKU, UPC in. Print-ready UPC-A label out.

Enter 11 digits and the check digit is added for you. Enter 12 and it is verified.

Columns can be in any order. Tab or comma separated. The SKU is read from the SKU-shaped value, the UPC from the 11 to 12 digit number, and the name from the rest. Two SKU-like values means the last one is used.

Paste your list and hit Preview list.

The PDF is a print-ready sheet of identical labels. Packaging fills in from the packaging reference based on the product name. Change it if the guess is wrong. Cases per carton 0 means the product ships carton-only.

Paste rows straight from your product spreadsheet (whole rows are fine), or name, SKU, UPC in any order. Include a category column matching SOURCE_TABS in app.js so packaging is picked by product line. Items without confirmed packaging show awaiting packaging until you type the quantities. Each ticked label gets a full sheet, or the copies set under Sheet layout.

Paste your list and hit Preview list.
Sheet layout (default Avery 5160 / 8160: 2.625 x 1 in, 30 per Letter sheet)

Labels are numbered left to right, top to bottom. Leave copies blank for a full sheet of each label. With copies set, labels in a batch follow each other on the same sheet.

Printing: print at Actual size (100%) with Fit to page turned off. Scaling shifts every row a little more than the one above it. The safe margin keeps text and digits clear of the label edges if the printer drifts slightly.
